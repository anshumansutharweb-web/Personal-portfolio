FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about working with AS Tech Studio—from project scoping and responsiveness to maintenance and agency partnerships.

What types of projects does AS Tech Studio accept? +

We specialize in custom business websites, high-converting landing pages, website redesigns, frontend web application development, and ongoing maintenance. Whether you are an ambitious startup needing an authoritative launch website or an established company looking to modernize your web presence, we deliver clean, tailored solutions built around your business goals.

Will my website be fully mobile responsive across all devices? +

Yes, absolutely. Every project is engineered mobile-first using modern CSS Grid and Flexbox. We rigorously test across modern iOS and Android smartphones, tablets, laptops, and ultra-wide desktop monitors to ensure responsive fluid layouts, zero horizontal overflow, touch-friendly navigation, and retina-crisp typography.

How do you calculate project estimates and project scope? +

Estimates are determined transparently based on the number of unique pages, custom interactive components, design complexity, content readiness, and your desired timeline. After an initial discovery review, we provide a detailed proposal outlining the exact scope, milestones, and a clear estimate—so you know exactly what to expect with zero hidden charges.

How do you collaborate with clients in India and internationally? +

Based in India (IST, UTC+5:30), we maintain structured communication workflows designed for smooth domestic and international collaboration. We schedule regular touchpoints with convenient timezone overlap across US East (EST), Gulf (GST), and European hours, complemented by clear asynchronous progress updates and live staging preview links.

Do you offer freelance or subcontracting support for digital agencies? +

Yes. We frequently partner with web design, creative, and digital marketing agencies seeking dependable freelance frontend development capacity. We integrate smoothly into your team's workflow, adhere strictly to your design specifications, write clean modular code, and deliver client projects reliably on schedule.

What happens after launch? Do you provide maintenance and updates? +

We stand behind our work. After launch, we provide initial post-deployment verification to guarantee everything runs without issues. For long-term peace of mind, we offer ongoing maintenance arrangements—covering content updates, bug fixes, performance monitoring, and layout improvements—available on an agreed ad-hoc or retainer basis.
